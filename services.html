Core Practice

Personal Injury

When negligence causes serious injury, you need a legal team that understands both the human impact and the legal complexities. DG Law Firm provides aggressive representation for all types of personal injury cases, from car accidents to catastrophic injuries.

We handle every aspect of your case—investigating the incident, gathering evidence, consulting experts, negotiating with insurance companies, and taking your case to trial if necessary. Our goal is maximum compensation for your injuries, lost wages, medical expenses, and pain and suffering.

Complex Litigation

Wrongful Death

Losing a loved one due to someone else's negligence is devastating. While no amount of money can bring back your family member, a wrongful death claim can provide financial security for surviving family members and hold the responsible parties accountable.

We understand the sensitive nature of these cases and work with compassion while pursuing aggressive legal action. Our attorneys help families recover damages for funeral expenses, loss of companionship, lost financial support, and the pain and suffering of their loved one.

Compassionate representation
Full damages recovery
Thorough investigation
Expert testimony coordination
Free Consultation
03
Specialized Practice

Medical Malpractice

Healthcare providers have a duty to meet the standard of care. When that duty is breached—through misdiagnosis, surgical errors, medication mistakes, or other negligence—patients suffer serious harm that demands accountability.

Medical malpractice cases are complex and require extensive medical knowledge. We work with qualified medical experts to evaluate your case, establish the standard of care, and prove how it was violated. Our thorough approach builds compelling cases that get results.

Expert medical review
Standard of care analysis
Hospital record analysis
Specialist expert witnesses
Free Consultation
04
Property Cases

Premises Liability

Property owners and managers have a legal duty to maintain safe conditions for visitors. When dangerous conditions—wet floors, inadequate security, poor lighting, structural defects—cause injury, the property owner may be liable.

We investigate premises liability cases thoroughly, documenting hazardous conditions, reviewing maintenance records, and identifying all responsible parties. From slip and fall accidents to inadequate security cases, we pursue full compensation for our clients.

Scene investigation
Maintenance record review
Security analysis
Expert reconstruction
Free Consultation
